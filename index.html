Sanatan Uploader

Sanatan Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Sanatan Beats / सनातन बीट्स. It uploads the channel's own devotional music videos to that channel and schedules them, using the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone else.
